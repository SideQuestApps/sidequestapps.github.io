The detail

What we collect, and what we do with it

01

What information we collect

We may collect non-personal information, such as device type, operating system, crash logs, performance diagnostics, and anonymous usage statistics.

No personal or sensitive data — names, email addresses, contact details — is collected unless you provide it directly for support or feedback.

02

How we use your data

The information collected is used solely to analyse app performance, troubleshoot issues, and refine the user experience.

The goal is simple: make the apps faster, steadier and more reliable for everyone using them.

03

Data sharing & sales

We do not sell your information to anyone. Data collected is never shared with third parties except as required for technical support or necessary app functionality (e.g. analytics services) — and only under strict confidentiality agreements.

04

Your choices & rights

You may opt out of diagnostics or analytics wherever settings permit.

If you have provided personal information for support, you have full rights to request deletion of that data at any time.

05

Policy updates

We may update this privacy policy periodically. Updates will be posted within the app and on our website, with version date revisions.

06

Who this covers

This policy applies to every SideQuest Apps release on the App Store — all of them — and to this website.

This page is a plain-language summary of how SideQuest Apps handles data. If anything here is unclear, ask — a privacy policy nobody can read is not much of a policy.

Questions about your data?

Ask directly and you will get a straight answer — including a deletion request, if that is what you need.
